# Strategy memo 2026

This memo sets the three priorities for the second half of 2026. It is the strategy we review with the board each quarter, and it replaces the earlier plan from January. Each priority has one owner, one number and one review date.

## Priority 1: launch the T2

The T2 is our next bike and the most important product decision of the year. Engineering owns the build, product owns the requirements, and sales and marketing own the plan that follows the launch. The launch plan lives in the product space and is reviewed at every leadership meeting.

## Priority 2: double the dealer network in DE/NL

We have 62 dealers across Germany, the Netherlands and Belgium. The goal is to double the network in Germany and the Netherlands by the end of next year. Elena owns the pipeline. Each new dealer goes through the same onboarding checklist, so that a dealer in Hamburg and a dealer in Utrecht get the same first week.

## Priority 3: reach 38% gross margin

Our T1 gross margin is 38% today, and we want to hold that as volume grows. Jonas owns the margin model. Price changes, supplier terms and assembly costs all go through that model before they are agreed.

## What we review

The board sees these three numbers every quarter, with the reasons behind any movement. Owners bring a short written update seven days before each review, and the leadership team decides what changes in the plan.
