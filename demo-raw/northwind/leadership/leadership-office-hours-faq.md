# Leadership office hours: FAQ

**When are office hours?** First Thursday of the month, 15:00 to 16:00 CET, in the Kreuzberg meeting room and on video for Amsterdam.

**Who can come?** Everyone at Northwind, from any department. You do not need an appointment.

**What can I ask?** Anything about direction, priorities, how decisions get made, or how your work connects to the company goals. Questions about your own pay or contract go to Hannah in People instead.

**Will my question be answered in the room?** If we know the answer, yes. If not, Mia writes it down and we answer in the all-hands notes within a week.

**Can I send a question beforehand?** Yes, send it to Mia Janssen. Anonymous questions are welcome through the suggestion box in the handbook.

**Where do I find past answers?** In the company core folder, under the all-hands notes. Decisions are in the leadership decisions note.

**Where does Northwind stand?** Model T1 is our current bike (500 Wh, EUR 2,190, 62 dealers in Germany, the Netherlands and Belgium). Model T2 is in development. 2025 revenue was EUR 9.8M.
