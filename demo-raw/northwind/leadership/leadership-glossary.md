# Glossary for company planning

- **Objective (O):** a direction for the year in one sentence, such as growing revenue.
- **Key result (KR):** a measurable number that shows progress on an objective.
- **OKR cycle:** we set company objectives once a year and review them every quarter.
- **All-hands:** the monthly company meeting, usually last Thursday of the month.
- **Decision note:** the short written record of what leadership decided, who owns it and by when.
- **Core folder:** notes everyone in the company may read.
- **Department core:** notes a department shares with the whole company, such as team charters and FAQs.
- **Department internal:** notes meant for the department itself.
- **Owner:** the single person answerable for a result. One owner per task.
- **Dealer:** an independent bike shop that sells Northwind bikes. We have 62 today.
- **T1 / T2:** our two e-bike models. T1 is on sale, T2 is in development.
- **Werkstatt Spree:** our contract assembly partner in Berlin.
- **Cellwerk:** our battery supplier. **Rahmenbau Nord:** our frame supplier.
