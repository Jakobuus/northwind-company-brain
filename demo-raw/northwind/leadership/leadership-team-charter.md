# Leadership team charter

The leadership team of Northwind Bikes is Clara Vogel (CEO and co-founder), Mia Janssen (Chief of Staff) and Niklas Roth (Head of Strategy and Corporate Development). We set the direction for a 29-person company with sites in Berlin Kreuzberg and Amsterdam.

## What we own
- Company strategy and the yearly objectives.
- The monthly all-hands and the written decisions note that follows each leadership meeting.
- Hiring plans at company level, together with People and Finance.
- Relationships with our battery and frame suppliers at executive level.

## How we work
- We meet every second Tuesday and publish decisions in the company core folder within two working days.
- A decision names an owner and a date. If nobody owns it, it is not decided.
- Questions for the team can go to any of the three of us. Mia coordinates the calendar and the follow-ups.

## What you can expect from us
- A straight answer on priorities, even when the answer is "not this quarter".
- No surprises at the all-hands: big news reaches the whole team at the same time.
- Open office hours on the first Thursday of each month in Kreuzberg and over video for Amsterdam.
