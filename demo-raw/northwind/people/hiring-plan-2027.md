# Hiring plan 2027

Written by Hannah Lindqvist, 18 October 2026. This plan follows the 2027 objectives agreed in the leadership
meetings, and it covers the roles we need to hit the first-half volume for the T1 and the T2 launch.

## Headcount

6 hires in 2027: 2 engineers, 2 sales, 1 operations, 1 customer care.

The two engineers sit in Product & Engineering and support firmware and the battery work. The two sales
hires go to Berlin and Amsterdam, one each, to cover the dealer growth in the Benelux market. The operations
hire joins Supply Chain to take over the supplier follow-up, and the customer care hire joins the German
support team.

## Timing

We open the roles in January so the first starts can land in the spring. Engineers take the longest, so
their search starts first. Sales and care can follow a few weeks later, once the job descriptions are
signed off by the department leads.

## Pay

No salaries are in this plan. Offers use the salary bands reference that the People team keeps up to date.
Any offer outside the band needs sign-off from Hannah and the hiring lead before it goes out.

## Open questions

- Do we hire the second Amsterdam account executive before the dealer count passes 70?
- Can operations use an agency for the first six months, or does the role need to be in-house from day one?
- Should customer care hire a bilingual agent first, given the Dutch ticket share?

I will bring the answers to the next People sync. Any change to headcount goes through Clara before it is
booked in the budget.
