Wiki: new joiner onboarding

Last updated 2026-03-10 by Ravi Shah (People Operations).

First-week checklist: laptop, accounts, buddy, brain access. Contact Ravi Shah.

Before day one

  • People Ops orders the laptop with the standard image for Berlin or Amsterdam.
  • Your lead names a buddy from the same department and tells them the start time.
  • Ravi sends the welcome email with the building, the desk and the first-day timetable.

Day one

  • Collect the laptop and set your password in the first session with IT.
  • Get access to email, chat, the code repository and the expense tool.
  • Lunch with your buddy, who walks you through the office and the kitchen rota.

Week one

  • Set up brain access: sign in with your work email, then read the company overview and the values page.
  • Book 30-minute intros with your lead and with two colleagues from other departments.
  • Friday check-in with Ravi to review the checklist and note anything missing.

Good to know

Your buddy is not your manager. Ask them about the small things: where the tools are, who to ask for a test bike, which meeting rooms book up first. Questions about pay, leave or the handbook go to the People team, and the handbook is in the people folder of the brain.
