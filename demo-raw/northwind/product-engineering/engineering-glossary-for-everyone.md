# Engineering glossary for non-engineers

- **Mid-drive motor:** a motor placed at the pedals, not in the wheel. It feels more natural and climbs better. T2 uses one.
- **Torque (Nm):** the pushing power of the motor. T2 has 85 Nm.
- **Wh (watt-hour):** the size of the battery. T1 has 500 Wh, T2 has 720 Wh. More Wh means more range.
- **BMS:** the battery management system, a small computer in the pack that protects the cells and reports their status. Ingrid is our expert.
- **Firmware:** the software inside the bike's motor controller and display.
- **Prototype:** a hand-built early version used for testing.
- **Pack:** the complete battery unit: cells, BMS and housing.
- **Range:** how far a bike travels on one charge. It changes with weight, hills, temperature and assist level.
- **Bench test / field test:** tests in the lab versus tests on real roads.
- **Service part:** a spare part dealers can order for repairs.

If a word is missing, ask in the engineering channel and we will add it here.
