# Product roadmap: summary for everyone

This is the version we are happy for the whole company to repeat to customers and partners.

## Now: Model T1
- On sale since April 2025, 500 Wh battery, retail EUR 2,190.
- 62 dealers in Germany, the Netherlands and Belgium.
- Ongoing work: small firmware improvements and service parts availability.

## Next: Model T2
- Mid-drive motor with 85 Nm torque.
- 720 Wh battery pack, a clear range step up from T1.
- Prototype testing continues through the winter. Dates for customers are announced by leadership and Marketing, not by individual teams.

## Later: ideas under review
- Better dealer diagnostics so workshops can read bike status quickly.
- Accessories such as a second battery and a rear rack.
- A simpler charger for dealer demo fleets.

## How to give input
Add your idea to the roadmap thread or tell Priya Nair directly. We review ideas on the first Friday of each month and answer every one.
