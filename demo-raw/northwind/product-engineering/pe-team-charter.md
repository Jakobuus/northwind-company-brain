# Product & Engineering team charter

We are Tobias Weber (Head of Engineering), Priya Nair (Product Manager), Ingrid Kowalski (battery and BMS), Felix Neumann (software) and Oskar Lindgren (mechanical).

## Mission
Build e-bikes people love to ride and dealers love to sell: reliable, serviceable and clearly better than the last model.

## What we own
- The Model T1 hardware and firmware, including fixes and updates.
- The Model T2 development: a mid-drive motor with 85 Nm torque and a 720 Wh battery pack.
- Technical specs, drawings and test reports.
- The product roadmap, owned by Priya.

## Principles
- Safety and battery quality come before schedule.
- We write down decisions about architecture so nobody has to guess later.
- We test on real bikes, not only on the bench.
- If a spec changes, Priya tells Sales, Marketing and Operations the same day.

## Working with us
Ask Priya for roadmap and feature questions, Tobias for technical feasibility, Ingrid for anything about batteries. We hold a short demo every other Friday that anyone may join.
