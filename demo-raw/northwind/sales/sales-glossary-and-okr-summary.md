# Sales glossary and OKR summary

## Words we use
- **SDR:** sales development rep. Finds and warms up new dealers. Daan and Mila.
- **AE:** account executive. Closes new dealers and grows existing ones. Lukas and Noah.
- **Pipeline:** all open dealer opportunities and their stage.
- **Quota:** the sales target for a person or region in a period.
- **Demo fleet:** bikes lent to a dealer so riders can test them.
- **Onboarding:** the first 60 days after a dealer signs: training, display set-up, first order.
- **Reorder rate:** share of dealers who order again within the year.

## OKR summary for 2027
- **Objective:** grow the dealer network without losing service quality.
- **KR 1:** add new dealers in Germany, the Netherlands and Belgium.
- **KR 2:** keep the reorder rate of existing dealers high.
- **KR 3:** every new dealer has a demo bike within 30 days of signing.

These support the company goal of revenue growth in 2027. Detailed targets live in the company OKRs.
