# Q3 sales review

## Headline numbers

In Q3 2026 we sold **1,480 T1 bikes**. Revenue for the quarter was **EUR 3.4M**, which includes the accessories we sold through dealers alongside the bikes. Cash from dealer payments came in on time for all but two accounts, and both have now paid.

## By market

- **Netherlands:** unit sales were up **22%** on Q2. Most of the growth came from the Randstad, where the two largest dealers each doubled their monthly orders after the spring demo events.
- **Germany:** steady. Berlin and Hamburg carried most of the volume. Brandenburg is still slow and needs a dedicated visit plan.
- **Belgium:** small but healthy. Antwerp is now our second-best dealer in the country by units.

## Dealer network

We added **4 new dealers** in the quarter, two in NL and two in DE. All four were onboarded with the standard checklist, and the first orders from three of them are already in the Q4 pipeline. We now have 62 active dealers in DE, NL and BE.

## What worked

- Demo bikes at the spring events converted well, especially when the dealer had a test ride booked the same week.
- Daan's follow-up calls within 48 hours of a demo gave the best close rate.
- Clear weekly order cut-off dates made it easier for dealers to plan their own stock.

## What we need to fix

- Dealers want firm delivery dates before they sign. We need to give them clearer planning dates in Q4.
- Quota tracking is still spread across three sheets. I will move it into one file before the Q4 review.

## Q4 focus

Close the open pipeline deals listed in the Q4 pipeline file, keep the NL growth going, and start the Brandenburg visit plan. Realistically, Q4 will be our biggest quarter so far, but only if we can give dealers reliable dates.
