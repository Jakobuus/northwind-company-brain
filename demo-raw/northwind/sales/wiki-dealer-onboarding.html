Five steps for every new dealer

  1. Signed agreement. The dealer signs the agreement and the credit check is complete before any order is booked.
  2. Kick-off call. Within five working days, the account executive walks the dealer through the T1 range, the order cut-off dates and the service basics.
  3. First order. Confirm the order form and the delivery window. Do not promise a delivery date before the order form is back.
  4. Demo bikes. Agree the demo bikes and the test ride schedule. Use the demo bike policy below.
  5. Review at 60 days. Check sell-through, the order pattern and any questions from the service desk.

Demo bike policy

A dealer gets 1 demo bike per 10 units ordered. Demo bikes stay with the dealer for the season and go back to us if the account closes. Ask Daan or Lukas before you release a demo bike outside this rule.
