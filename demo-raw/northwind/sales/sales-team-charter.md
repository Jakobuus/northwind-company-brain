# Sales team charter

Sales at Northwind is seven people in two regions. Jakob Volbracht (Head of Sales) leads the team. Elena Costa leads DACH and Sanne Visser leads Benelux. Lukas Hartmann (Berlin) and Noah Becker (Munich) are account executives. Daan de Vries (Amsterdam) and Mila Jansen (Rotterdam) are SDRs.

## Mission
Help independent bike dealers sell more Northwind bikes with less effort, and keep every dealer happy enough to reorder.

## What we own
- The dealer network: today 62 dealers in Germany, the Netherlands and Belgium.
- Pipeline, quotas and forecasts for new dealers.
- Dealer terms, demo fleets and onboarding.
- Feedback from dealers back to Product and Marketing.

## How we work
- Weekly pipeline review on Monday morning.
- Every deal has an owner and a next step with a date.
- We keep the CRM current, because Finance and Operations plan from it.

## Working with us
Dealer enquiries go to the regional lead. Colleagues who meet a dealer or a shop owner should pass the contact to us rather than quoting terms themselves.
