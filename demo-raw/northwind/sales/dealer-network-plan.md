# Dealer network plan 2026 to 2027

## Where we are

We finish Q3 2026 with **62 active dealers** in Germany, the Netherlands and Belgium. Most of them sell the T1 and carry a demo bike for test rides. The network grew by four dealers in Q3, and the Q4 pipeline has more open deals than we can serve well with the current team.

## Where we want to be

By the end of 2027 we want **100 dealers in DE and NL**. Belgium stays opportunistic: we will take good dealers there, but we will not push for coverage targets.

## Priorities by region

1. **Netherlands, Randstad.** Most of our growth so far. Fill the gaps between Utrecht, Haarlem and Delft first, because a dealer there can be served in a single day trip.
2. **Germany, Berlin and Brandenburg.** Lukas owns the Berlin accounts. Brandenburg has room for about ten more dealers, but each one needs a visit plan before we sign.
3. **Netherlands, north and south.** Groningen and Eindhoven are proven. Two or three more dealers in each would give us regional coverage.
4. **Germany, Hamburg, Bremen and Cologne.** Good dealers with strong service workshops. We should sign them as the team grows, not earlier.

## How we get there

- Keep the same onboarding checklist for every new dealer, so each one gets the same contacts, the same demo policy and the same first-order support.
- Run one regional demo event per quarter in each priority region.
- Review dealer discount terms every quarter so the network stays profitable, and do not change them mid-season.
- Track the account list in the pipeline file, and move each dealer through the stages every week.

## Open questions for the team

- Who covers the second Berlin territory once the Berlin accounts pass twenty dealers?
- Do we add a dealer manager role in 2027, or does the sales team keep owning accounts?

I will bring a first version of the regional split to the next sales meeting. Please send your views on priorities before then.
