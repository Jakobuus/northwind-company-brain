# Dealer FAQ for colleagues

**What is a dealer?** An independent bike shop that buys Northwind bikes and sells them to riders.

**How many do we have?** 62, in Germany, the Netherlands and Belgium.

**What do dealers sell today?** Model T1, a 500 Wh e-bike with a retail price of EUR 2,190.

**A shop owner asks me about terms. What do I say?** Thank them and pass the contact to Sales. Do not quote prices, discounts or delivery dates. Elena covers Germany, Austria and Switzerland, Sanne covers the Benelux.

**A customer asks where to buy a bike.** Send them to the dealer finder on the website.

**A dealer has a complaint or a repair question.** Send it to Customer Care, and let the regional lead know.

**How can I help Sales?** Introduce us to shops you know, join a dealer visit, or share what you hear from riders. Ask Daan or Mila if you want to go on a call.

**Where do I find the dealer list?** Ask your regional lead. It lives in the CRM.
