# Company handbook: who we are

Northwind Bikes is a 29-person e-bike maker: Berlin Kreuzberg (HQ, 22 people) and Amsterdam (sales and design, 7 people), founded 2021.

Our job is simple. We design and sell e-bikes that people ride every day, and we build the service around them. Berlin runs engineering, operations, finance and most of the company. Amsterdam runs the Dutch and Belgian sales work and the design studio.

## What we sell

Model T1 launched April 2025, retail EUR 2,190, sold in DE/NL/BE through 62 dealers and online. Dealers carry the bike in store, and riders can also order it online with delivery to their door. Assembly is done by our contract partner Werkstatt Spree in Berlin, and the frame and the battery come from our suppliers.
