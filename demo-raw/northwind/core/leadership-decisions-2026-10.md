# Leadership decisions, October 2026

These are the decisions the leadership team has taken this month. Each one stays binding until a later decision replaces it, and each one is filed here so nobody has to ask around for it.

## Decision 1: Model T2 launch date

I am setting the launch of the Model T2 for 15 March 2027, and this date is final. Product, sales and marketing plans should be built around it from now on. If a team sees a real risk to the date, it raises the risk with me and with Tobias, in writing, so that we can decide together.

## Filing and budget

One more decision: the T2 teaser campaign budget is capped at EUR 40k. Marketing owns the spend and reports it each month to Jonas, and any change above the cap comes back to this note.

## Why we write decisions down

A decision that lives only in a meeting gets remembered differently by each person in the room. Writing it here, with the date and the owner, means the next person who asks gets the same answer. If you disagree with a decision, say so in the department channel and bring the argument to the next leadership meeting. Do not quietly work around it.
