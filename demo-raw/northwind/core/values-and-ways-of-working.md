# Our values and ways of working

We wrote these down in May so that new people and old hands work from the same page. They are not slogans. If a decision conflicts with them, the decision gets questioned.

## Values

Four values: build for the rider, be straight, own the outcome, ship small.

Build for the rider means every change starts with a person riding one of our bikes, not with a spec sheet. Be straight means we say bad news early, in plain words, to the person who can act on it. Own the outcome means you follow your piece of work until the rider or dealer actually has the result, not just until the ticket closes. Ship small means a smaller change that works beats a big plan that waits.

## Meetings

Meetings: default 25 minutes, written notes for every decision, decisions are filed in the brain. The owner of a meeting sends the notes within one working day, and the notes list the decision, the owner and the date it was made.

If a meeting does not need a decision, it does not need to be a meeting. Ask for a written update instead. Long calls are for hard conversations and for the people who really need to be in the room.

## Working in two cities

Berlin and Amsterdam work on the same calendar and the same notes. Put your time zone next to your name in the calendar when you travel, and record decisions in the shared space even if the people who need them are in the other office.
