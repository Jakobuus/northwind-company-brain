# Content calendar Q4 2026

Owner: Marta Silva (Content Marketer). Agreed with Lea Bauer on 2 October 2026.

## Rhythm

- Social posts go out every week, two per channel (Instagram and Facebook).
- Two videos a month on YouTube. One is a ride report, one is a workshop or product explainer.
- The newsletter goes to riders once a month, at the start of the month.

## November

The big story this quarter is a customer story. We follow one rider from Amsterdam who commutes on a T1 all year. The story runs in the November newsletter, as a video, and as three posts. Marta writes the text, Ben books the interview and the filming day.

The November campaign is the T1 winter push. It uses the same look as the brand guidelines and nothing about the T2 yet.

## December

- Holiday gift guide with accessories only.
- One video on winter riding safety.
- Teaser planning for the T2 campaign starts on 1 December. Lea owns the brief. Marta has no T2 material before then.
