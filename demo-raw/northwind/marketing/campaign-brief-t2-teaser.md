# T2 teaser campaign brief

Owner: Lea Bauer (Head of Marketing). Written 20 October 2026. Status: draft for review with Elena and Ben.

## Goal

Build awareness of the Model T2 among existing T1 riders and among people who have already looked at our dealers. We are not selling the T2 yet. The aim is a waiting list and a warm audience for the launch.

## Budget and timing

- Teaser budget: EUR 40k in total.
- The campaign runs from January to March 2027.
- Creative is produced in December so that the first posts can go out in the first week of January.

## Channels

1. Instagram: short vertical clips of the T2 prototype and the engineering team at work.
2. YouTube: two longer videos, one on the mid-drive and one on the 720 Wh pack.
3. Dealer windows: printed teaser posters in the 62 partner shops in Germany, the Netherlands and Belgium. Sales (Elena Costa) agrees the placement with each dealer.

## Message

Keep the message to what we can show. The T2 is in development. We talk about the ride and the engineering, not about the price. There is no price in this campaign and nobody on the team quotes one outside Finance.

## Measures

- Waiting list sign-ups from the landing page.
- Video views and watch time on the two YouTube films.
- Visits to the dealer locator from the dealer window posters, tracked with a code on each poster.

- Priya confirms which prototype shots are safe to publish.
- Marta reviews every asset against the brand guidelines before it goes out.
