# Brand voice quick guide

## Who we are
Northwind makes honest, well-built e-bikes in Berlin and Amsterdam. We sound like a good friend who knows bikes.

## Four rules
1. **Plain.** Short sentences, everyday words. No jargon where a simple word works.
2. **Specific.** Say "500 Wh battery" and not "long range". Use numbers we can back up.
3. **Warm.** Talk to one rider, not to a crowd.
4. **Honest.** If something is not ready, we say so. We never promise a date that has not been announced.

## Facts you may use
- Model T1: 500 Wh battery, retail EUR 2,190.
- 62 dealers in Germany, the Netherlands and Belgium.
- Founded 2021, based in Berlin Kreuzberg and Amsterdam.

## Please avoid
- Superlatives such as "the best e-bike ever".
- Claims about Model T2 that have not been released by Marketing.
- Comparing us with named competitors.

## Languages
We write in English, German and Dutch. Ask Marta for a native check before publishing.
