# Marketing team charter

Marketing is Lea Bauer (Head of Marketing), Marta Silva (Content Marketer) and Ben Okafor (Growth Marketer).

## Mission
Make Northwind the bike brand riders in Germany, the Netherlands and Belgium think of first, and give dealers material that helps them sell.

## What we own
- Brand guidelines, logo and tone of voice.
- The website and the newsletter.
- Campaigns, content and social channels.
- Dealer marketing kits.

## How we work
- A content calendar per quarter, shared with the company.
- Every public message about a product is checked with Product before it goes out.
- Every campaign has one goal, one owner and one number we measure.

## Working with us
- Need a visual, text or a slot in the newsletter? Send a short brief to Marta.
- Ideas for growth experiments go to Ben.
- Anything about brand rules goes to Lea.
- Please do not publish product claims on your own channels without asking us first.
