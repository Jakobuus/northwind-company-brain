# Marketing FAQ and glossary

## FAQ
**Can I post about Northwind on LinkedIn?** Yes, please do. Stick to public facts and our brand voice guide.

**Who approves a press or partner request?** Send it to Lea, do not answer yourself.

**Where do I find logos and templates?** In the brand guidelines deck. Ask Marta if you cannot find it.

**How do I get a slot in the newsletter?** Send Marta a three-line brief by the 10th of the month before.

**What does Marketing do for dealers?** Dealer kits with posters, product sheets and social images.

## Glossary
- **Campaign:** a time-limited effort with one goal and one number.
- **Teaser:** early content that builds interest before a product is announced.
- **CTR:** click-through rate, the share of people who click.
- **Conversion:** a visitor doing what we want, like requesting a test ride.
- **Organic / paid:** traffic we earn versus traffic we buy.
- **Content calendar:** the schedule of what we publish and when.
- **Brand guidelines:** the rules for logo, colours and tone.
