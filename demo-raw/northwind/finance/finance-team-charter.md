# Finance team charter

Finance is Jonas Brandt (CFO), Noor Haddad (Financial Analyst) and Carl Ekstrom (Controller).

## Mission
Keep Northwind financially healthy and give everyone the numbers they need to decide well.

## What we own
- Monthly close and the quarterly report.
- The yearly budget and the cash forecast.
- Invoicing, payments and supplier payment runs.
- Expense and purchasing rules.

## Calendar
- Month-end close: first five working days of the month.
- Budget requests for the next year: collected in October.
- Quarterly review with leadership after each close.

## Working with us
- Send purchase requests above the limit in the expense policy to Carl before you order.
- Send budget questions to Noor.
- Ask Jonas for anything that needs a decision on spending or financing.
- We will always tell you why a request is declined and what would change the answer.

## Where we are
The company is financially healthy: 2025 revenue was EUR 9.8M, and Model T1 has a gross margin of 38%.
