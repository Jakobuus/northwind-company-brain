# Finance glossary and yearly calendar

## Glossary
- **Revenue:** money from selling bikes and accessories. Q3 2026 revenue was EUR 3.4M.
- **Gross margin:** revenue minus the direct cost of making the product, as a share of revenue. For Model T1 it is 38%.
- **Cash:** money in the bank. It is a different thing from profit.
- **Budget:** the plan for spending and income in a year.
- **Forecast:** our best current estimate, updated as facts change.
- **Close:** the monthly process of checking and locking the books.
- **Accrual:** a cost or income booked in the month it belongs to, even if the payment moves later.

## Yearly calendar
- **January:** annual numbers and audit preparation.
- **April, July, October:** quarterly reviews with leadership.
- **October:** budget requests from departments.
- **November:** draft budget for next year.
- **December:** budget approved and shared with department leads.
