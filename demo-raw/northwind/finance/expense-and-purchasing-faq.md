# Expense and purchasing FAQ

**How do I claim travel or other expenses?** Submit receipts in the expense tool within 30 days. Carl approves them weekly.

**What can I buy without asking?** Small office and software items up to the limit in the expense policy. Above that, ask Carl first.

**Who pays for team events?** Each department has a small yearly allowance. Ask your department lead before you plan.

**How do suppliers get paid?** Operations confirms delivery, Finance pays on the agreed terms. If a supplier is waiting, ask Carl.

**Where do I find invoices?** In the finance shared folder. Ask Noor for access to a specific one.

**Who do I ask about budgets?** Noor prepares the numbers, Jonas decides. Department leads submit requests in October.

**What if I overspend by mistake?** Tell Carl early. We fix mistakes quickly and without blame.

**Can I get a company card?** Department leads can ask Jonas. Cards come with a monthly limit.
