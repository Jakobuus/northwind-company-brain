# T2 pricing memo 1 (10 Sep)

Fact key: subject model-t2, attribute retail-price.

The Model T2 (mid-drive 85 Nm, 720 Wh pack) needs a retail price before the dealer briefings in October. Finance builds the number from cost first. This is the first pass, and I want it on file before Sales starts quoting.

Cost-plus view: unit cost EUR 1,720, a 45% markup gives EUR 2,494. That is the number I can defend line by line, because every part of it comes from the bill of materials and the assembly quote from Werkstatt Spree.

I am setting the retail price of the Model T2 at EUR 2,490. The price sits just under the cost-plus figure, which is a deliberate choice: it is a clean price point that dealers can quote without a discount conversation, and it keeps the launch offer simple.

What this memo does not cover: market research. The competitor and rider data is not in yet, so this price is a cost-based starting point and nothing more. Once the survey is back I will check the price against it in a second memo.
