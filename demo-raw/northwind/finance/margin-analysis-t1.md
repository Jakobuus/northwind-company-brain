# T1 margin analysis

Prepared by Noor Haddad, Financial Analyst, for the Q4 pricing review.

## Summary

T1 gross margin 38% by channel (dealer 34%, online 44%). The blended figure is what the board sees, but the channel split is what drives decisions about dealer terms and online promotions.

## By channel

Dealer sales carry a lower margin because the dealer keeps a share of the list price and some of the dealer support cost comes out of our side. Online sales keep more of the list price, which is why the online margin is ten points higher. The gap has stayed roughly the same since the first dealer contracts were signed in 2025.

The channel mix moved toward online in the summer. That lifted the blended margin by about half a point, even though the dealer margin itself did not change.

## Material cost trend

Material cost per bike rose slightly over the last two quarters. Most of the increase came from the battery cells and frame tubing. Labour and assembly rates were flat, as the assembly contract has a fixed rate until the end of the year.

We cannot yet attribute the cost increase to a single supplier, so the variance is shown at the bill-of-materials level in the appendix sheet.

## What this means for the T1

At the current mix, the T1 is healthy. A further move toward online would raise the margin, but it would also shift support load to Customer Care, which is not priced into the current model. I would not change dealer terms until we have the support cost per channel.

## Next steps

- Build the support cost per channel with Customer Care.
- Rerun the channel margin with the Q3 actuals once the close is final.
- Keep the T1 price list unchanged for the autumn season.
