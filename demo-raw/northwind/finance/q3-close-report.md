# Q3 close report

Prepared by Carl Ekstrom, Controller. Figures are from the closed Q3 2026 ledger.

## Headline numbers

Q3 revenue EUR 3.4M, gross margin 38%, cash EUR 7.9M at quarter end.

Revenue came from 1,480 T1 bikes plus accessories. The T1 remains the volume product: it has been on sale since the 2025 launch and is sold through 62 dealers in Germany, the Netherlands and Belgium, plus our own online channel.

## Revenue and margin

Gross margin held at 38% for the quarter. Mix moved a little toward online sales in September, which helped the blended figure. Dealer margins are unchanged from the summer agreements.

Accessories sold in line with the plan. Helmets and lock sets were the strongest lines, and the returns rate on accessories stayed low.

## Cash

Cash closed the quarter at EUR 7.9M. Payments to suppliers were on the normal terms. The working capital build for the T2 pilot builds has not started yet, so it does not show in Q3 numbers.

## Open items for Q4

- Reconcile the September dealer invoices with the sales ledger before the November close.
- Confirm the accrual for the firmware support contract with Product & Engineering.
- Update the fixed asset register for the Amsterdam design studio equipment.
