# My doubts about the launch

Written for myself. Not for the team yet.

## The battery requirement

I doubt that the removable battery requirement fits the March 2027 launch and I want to check with Oskar before I tell Tobias.

## What I know

- The removable pack adds a latch and a seal to the frame, and Oskar has not costed the second tooling round.
- Ingrid's BMS work assumes a fixed pack, so the wiring would change too.
- T2 is still listed for 15 March 2027 in every plan I have seen.

## What I need before Tobias sees it
