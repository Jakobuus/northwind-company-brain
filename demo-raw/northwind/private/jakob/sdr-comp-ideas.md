# SDR reward ideas (private, thinking only)

Nothing here is decided and none of it has gone to People or Finance.

## Why I am looking at it
Daan in Amsterdam and Mila in Rotterdam do the first calls. Today it is hard to see what each of them adds, because the deal is closed by an account executive.

## Ideas
1. **Reward meetings that happen.** Not calls made. A booked meeting only counts if the dealer actually turns up.
2. **Small team goal on top.** If the SDR team helps open a new dealer, everyone shares in it. This avoids the two competing over leads.
3. **Quality check.** Lukas or Noah rate each lead from 1 to 3 so we do not reward weak leads.
4. **Quarterly review.** Adjust after one quarter, not mid-quarter.

## Risks
- Too complex to explain in one minute.
- Could push SDRs towards easy leads only.

## Next steps
Talk to Daan and Mila about what they would find fair. Then take a one-page version to Hannah in People for advice before anything is written down.
