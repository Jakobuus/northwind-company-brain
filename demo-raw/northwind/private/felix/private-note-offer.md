# Outside offer

I have an offer from a Munich startup, I would ask for EUR 78k, and I do not trust the pay review.

Writing this down so I stop going round in circles. The work here is good. The OTA updater I built this year is the best thing I have shipped, and the bike team is small enough that I can see the whole stack from the bench to the app. The Munich people want me on the firmware side too, and they were honest that their process is rougher and their hours are longer.

The pay review has been open for a while and I have heard nothing concrete since the last round. Hannah was kind about it when I asked, but kind is not a number. I do not want to ask for a raise by email and then sit waiting for weeks, and I do not want to be the person who leaves because nobody answered.

What I need to decide is whether I stay through the next T2 bench cycle or leave before it. I do not want to make that call on a bad week, so I will not decide this month.

Next steps:

1. Write down what I would need to hear from Tobias to stay, in plain words.
2. Get the exact terms the startup has put on paper, including the notice period and the equity split.
3. Talk to one friend who has made a similar move in the last two years.

I will not bring this up at standup or in the team chat until I have decided.
