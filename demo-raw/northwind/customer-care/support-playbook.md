# Support playbook

Owner: Yara Kamal, Head of Customer Care. This is how Care answers every rider contact, whether it comes by email, the app chat or the phone line. Agents read it on their first day and before every shift change.

## The short version

First reply within 4 hours, escalate battery issues to Engineering via Yara, refunds up to EUR 150 without approval.

## Response targets

Every new ticket gets a first reply within 4 hours during business hours (Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 CET). A ticket that arrives after 18:00 gets its first reply before noon the next working day. We answer in the language the rider writes in. Pieter Bos covers the Dutch queue and Greta Fuchs covers the German queue, and either of them can pick up the other's tickets when the queue is long.

A reply is a real answer or a clear next step with a date. "We are looking into it" does not count as a first reply on its own.

## Battery issues

Care does not diagnose battery faults. When a rider reports a battery warning, a pack that will not charge, or any swelling or heat, the agent does four things:

1. Asks the rider to stop charging and keep the bike away from heat sources.
2. Collects the frame serial number, the firmware version shown in the app and a photo of the error screen.
3. Opens an Engineering escalation note on the ticket and sends it to Yara.
4. Tells the rider what happens next and when they will hear back.

Yara forwards every battery escalation to Engineering the same working day. Engineering answers the escalation, and the agent passes the answer to the rider in their own words. The ticket stays open until the rider confirms the bike is working or has been collected.

## Refunds

Agents can refund up to EUR 150 without approval. The refund goes back to the original payment method and is logged on the ticket with the reason code. Anything above EUR 150 goes to Yara, who decides within one working day. Do not promise a refund in writing before the decision is in.

## Closing a ticket

A ticket is resolved when the rider confirms the problem is fixed or when five working days pass with no reply from the rider. Do not close a ticket while an escalation to Engineering is still open.
