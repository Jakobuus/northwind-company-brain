# CSAT report Q3 2026

Prepared by Ines Moreau, Care Analyst, for Yara Kamal and the Customer Care team.

## Headline

CSAT 4.4 out of 5, NPS 41, first response 3.2 hours on average.

The first response figure is the average across all 412 tickets in the quarter. It is inside the 4-hour target in the support playbook, but the average hides a spread: app pairing tickets wait longest, and accessories tickets are answered fastest.

## What riders rate highly

Riders who had a delivery damage case and got a fast replacement rate their experience well above average, often with a comment about the packaging. Accessories orders also score well, with 95 percent of tickets resolved on first contact.

## Where scores drop

App pairing is the weakest category. It has the most tickets after "other", the slowest first response of any category and the lowest resolution rate. Most of these riders answer the survey after a second or third contact, and their comments mention the same steps being repeated.

Battery range is the second concern. Riders in the Netherlands write more about cold-weather range than riders in Germany, and the scores in that group are lower by a small margin.

## Survey method

The survey goes out by email within 24 hours of a ticket closing. About one rider in three answers. Responses are anonymous to agents, and Ines reviews the free-text comments each week before they go into this report.

## Next steps for Q4

Yara and Ines agreed three things for the next quarter. Care will publish a shorter app pairing guide with the first reply, so riders do not have to repeat the steps in chat. The team will track CSAT per category, not only in total. And the weekly comment review will flag any cluster of similar complaints to Yara the same day, so that a pattern reaches Engineering faster than a quarterly report would take.
