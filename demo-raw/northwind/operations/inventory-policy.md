# Inventory policy

Owner: Sofia Marchetti, COO. Applies to the Berlin and Amsterdam stock locations. Reviewed each June.

## Purpose

We hold just enough stock to build the bikes we have promised to dealers, and no more. Cash sits in batteries and frames on a shelf, so every extra part is a cost. The rules below are meant to stop us from running out of a critical part in the middle of a build week, without buying in bulk out of worry.

## Battery packs

Safety stock for battery packs is 3 weeks of planned production. The planner, Tomasz Nowak, recalculates the figure whenever the production plan changes by more than a week's worth of bikes.

Battery packs come from Cellwerk GmbH. Their lead time is longer than the other parts, so the safety stock sits on top of the normal reorder point and is never used to cover a planned shortfall. If stock drops into the safety band, Mateo Fischer raises the purchase order the same day and tells Sofia.

## Frames and motors

Reorder rules for frames and motors are simpler. Frames from Rahmenbau Nord are reordered at the reorder point shown on the stock sheet, with the quantity set to the next two build weeks. Motors follow the same logic with the quantities agreed in the framework order.

## Counting and ownership

- Cycle counts run every Friday in Berlin and every second Friday in Amsterdam.
- Any gap larger than two units is logged and reported to Sofia before the next build.
- Aylin Demir owns the stock sheet and the transfer log between the two sites.

## Changes

Changes to this policy go through Sofia. Mateo and Tomasz can propose a change in the operations meeting, but the new rule only takes effect once it is written here with a new date at the top.
